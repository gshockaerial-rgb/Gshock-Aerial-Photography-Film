Frequently asked questions

What to know before booking

What tower types can be documented?

Gshock Aerial can capture imagery for cellular, water, transmission and other tower structures, subject to site and airspace requirements.

Does a drone inspection replace an engineer?

No. Aerial imagery supports inspection and documentation, while engineering conclusions should be made by appropriately qualified professionals.

What deliverables are available?

Deliverables can include detailed still photos, video and mapped visual documentation tailored to the inspection scope.
