Frequently asked questions

What to know before booking

What information is included in a floor plan?

Floor plans can show room layout, approximate dimensions, doors and windows, with additional options available by project.

Are floor plans suitable for construction?

Marketing floor plans are intended for property presentation and general spatial understanding, not as architectural or construction documents.

Can floor plans be bundled with other media?

Yes. Floor plans can be combined with aerial photography, video and 360-degree tours for a complete property media package.
